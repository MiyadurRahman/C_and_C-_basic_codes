Area of Different Shapes:
Triangle

Area = ½ × base × height
b = base
h = vertical height
Rectangle

Area = width × height
w = width
h = height
Trapezoid (US) / Trapezium (UK)

Area = ½ × (a + b) × h
a and b = lengths of parallel sides
h = vertical height
Ellipse

Area = π × a × b
a and b = lengths of semi-major and semi-minor axes
Square

Area = a²
a = length of a side
Parallelogram

Area = base × height
b = base
h = vertical height
Circle

Area = π × r²
Circumference = 2 × π × r
r = radius
Sector

Area = ½ × r² × θ
r = radius
θ = angle in radians
Note:
h is at right angles to b.
